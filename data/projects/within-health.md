# Within Health

## Overview

Within Health is a radiology follow-up management platform developed to help ensure that important findings and follow-up recommendations in radiology reports are not missed or delayed.

The platform uses AI and Natural Language Processing (NLP) to identify follow-up recommendations from radiology reports and track them through their required follow-up period. It helps radiology practices manage these recommendations and automate communication with patients and referring physicians through reminders and outreach.

The system provides visibility into the complete follow-up process from identifying a recommendation to tracking its status and confirming that the required action has been taken. By helping close this communication and care loop, the platform aims to support timely follow-up, early clinical action, and better patient outcomes.

## Features

- AI-Based Recommendation Detection – Identifies follow-up recommendations from radiology reports using AI and Natural Language Processing (NLP)
- Follow-Up Tracking – Tracks recommendations from the time they are identified through completion or resolution
- Patient & Physician Outreach – Automates reminders and communications to patients and referring physicians when follow-up is required
- Follow-Up Queue – Provides a centralized view of recommendations categorized by statuses such as overdue, coming due, non-compliant, and adherent
- Patient Details & Timeline – Displays patient information, recommendation history, communication activities, and follow-up events in a timeline
- Multiple Communication Channels – Supports outreach through SMS, email, fax, phone calls, and print
- Recommendation Status Management – Helps care teams monitor and update the status of follow-up recommendations
- Search & Filtering – Enables users to quickly find patients and recommendations using details such as patient name, MRN, phone number, and accession number
- Reporting & Visibility – Provides summary counts and dashboards to help teams monitor follow-up performance and outstanding recommendations
- Closed-Loop Follow-Up – Ensures recommendations are actively tracked until the required follow-up action is completed
